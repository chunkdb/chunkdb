- The WATCH CATALOG epoch and revision survive restarts: a new directory feature `catalog_revision` (incompat bit `0x80`, enabled on new directories, effective from the next start after ENABLE FEATURE) keeps them in `chunkdb.catalog`, marked in progress when a catalog statement reaches its decision and recording its revision before the revision is published, so a start after a crash during a statement moves to a new revision and one revision never names two catalog states; every catalog statement that reaches its decision takes a revision, a revision without a change a watch can see arrives as `[progress, epoch, revision]`, and `WATCH CATALOG AFTER epoch revision` continues from the current revision or one the server still holds, else sends `resync` and the whole catalog; backups leave the record out, so a restored directory starts a new epoch, and `chunkdb_verify` checks it (#146).
