- Add the BLOCKS selection `BLOCKS x0 y0 [z0] TO x1 y1 [z1]` in block coordinates to GET, AGGREGATE and SCAN CHUNKS. It covers the chunks that hold any of its blocks and keeps only its blocks. GET AREA and GET BLOCKS take WHERE. SCAN CHUNKS takes AREA, AREA AROUND and BLOCKS and returns only chunks in the selection. LIMIT can count excluded candidates in the selection's x range, and the reply adds `after` as with WHERE. With WHERE or BLOCKS, forms hold only matching blocks, and GET and scans without SINCE omit chunks with no matching blocks. A SINCE scan with WHERE or BLOCKS requires WITH DATA and returns null data for changed chunks with no matching blocks. HELLO adds the feature `block_selection` (#145).
