- Add partitioned slots: `CREATE SLOT 's' ON t ... PARTITIONS n` splits a slot into 1 to 256 partitions by a fixed hash of the chunk coordinates, and `WATCH t SLOT 's' PARTITION p, ...` holds some of them, so several watches share one slot; each change of a chunk goes to the watch holding its partition, a change of several partitions reaches each watch with its own chunks under the same revision, and a watch without PARTITION holds every partition. Each partition keeps its own acknowledged position: ACK saves it for the watch's partitions, a watch starts at the lowest of its partitions and skips what each acknowledged, and SHOW SLOTS adds `partitions`, one `{partition, acked, attached}` map per partition, with `acked` the lowest of them. A watch asking for a held partition receives SLOT_BUSY, and TAKEOVER replaces the one watch holding them. `chunkdb.slots` gains header flag `0x4` with a partition count for each slot and a position and resync flag for each partition, owned by the new table feature `slot_partitions` (incompat bit `0x100`), which new directories enable. Existing slots keep their saved positions, and MIGRATE refuses PARTITIONS. HELLO adds the `slot_partitions` capability. CREATE SLOT refuses with OUT_OF_RANGE a slot that would take the table's slot records past 1024 slots or 1 MiB, about 450 slots of 256 partitions, instead of failing as damaged records (#156).
