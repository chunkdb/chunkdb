- Add `WATCH AREA cx0 cy0 TO cx1 cy1 [, AREA ...]` for a streaming table watch without SLOT. It replaces the watch's areas and replies `{epoch, revision}` after the old-area pushes, including buffered CHUNKS pushes. Later pushes carry changes after that revision in the new areas, and the watch shares projected events with watches that have the new filters. With no open watch, on a slot or catalog watch (`reason=slot_filters` for a slot), or with axes other than the table's, it receives INVALID_ARGUMENT and any open watch keeps its areas. A full WATCH on a watching connection still receives PROTOCOL and closes. A reply to a line read on the stream is preserved if a lagging watch receives `resync`. HELLO adds feature `watch_area_replace` (#146).
