- New data directories, tables and standalone stores enable `commit_groups` and bind a scope to an INACTIVE `chunkdb.groups.owner` root, so earlier builds refuse them. `ENABLE FEATURE 'commit_groups'` binds an existing directory and its tables. Backups carry the owner root, and restores validate it and bind restored tables to the destination scope. `chunkdb_verify --data-dir` checks the scopes and root of a catalog or standalone store before replaying chunks.
