- SCAN CHUNKS takes `AREA cx0 cy0 [cz0] TO cx1 cy1 [cz1]` or `AREA AROUND cx cy [cz] RADIUS r` after the table and lists only the chunks inside it, in the same ascending order. These selectors work with SINCE, WHERE and WITH DATA, and in a READ ONLY transaction when SINCE is omitted. Each page seeks through the coordinate index one column at a time, and AFTER may lie outside the area (#147).
